# Business Case & Go-to-Market Strategy — Project Udyam Digital
**10-Slide Hackathon Executive Presentation**
*Accel Business | Airtel SYNC Hackathon 2026*

---

### Slide 1: Title & Executive Summary
- **Title:** Project Udyam Digital — Making Small Businesses Go Digital in One Sitting.
- **The Core Opportunity:** India's 6.3 crore MSMEs are digitizing rapidly, yet enterprise telecom acquisition remains trapped in slow, manual processes.
- **The Accel Problem:** 5,000 leads $\rightarrow$ 368 activations (7.36% conversion), 17.6-day activation cycle, ₹15,000 CAC, and 3.0 customer status chase calls.
- **The Solution:** A unified AI-driven digital acquisition platform delivering personalized bundles, instant quotes, automated digital KYC, and real-time activation tracking.

---

### Slide 2: The Customer Problem & Funnel Leakage
- **Manual Discovery:** Sales reps spend days identifying basic connectivity needs; 32% of generated leads are never contacted.
- **The 56% Quote Cliff:** 1,974 needs assessed yield only 861 accepted quotes. Businesses drop off waiting 3–5 days for pricing proposals.
- **Paperwork Paralysis:** 32% of customers who accept quotes drop out during KYC due to physical document pickup and opaque rejection reasons.
- **Fragmented Billing:** Fiber broadband, business SIMs, Cloud POS, and CCTV are billed separately, creating operational friction.

---

### Slide 3: Target Customer Segments & Market Prioritization
- **Segment 1: Micro Retail & Services (55% of leads, ₹2,500/mo avg)**
  - *Strategy:* Zero-touch, self-serve mobile flow with 5G AirFiber (`P04`) + Cloud POS (`P19`) + Postpaid SIM (`P07`).
- **Segment 2: Professional Offices (25% of leads, ₹5,000/mo avg)**
  - *Strategy:* The profit engine. High-speed fiber (`P02`/`P03`) + Cloud Email (`P16`) + Backup (`P17`) + CCTV (`P23`).
- **Segment 3: Small Multi-Site Retail (15% of leads, ₹12,800/mo avg)**
  - *Strategy:* SD-WAN (`P10`) + Failover (`P09`) + Consolidated billing across branches.
- **Segment 4: Small Manufacturers (5% of leads, ₹18,500/mo avg)**
  - *Strategy:* Dedicated Leased Line (`P05`/`P06`) + IoT machine monitoring (`P24`).

---

### Slide 4: The Core Experience — AI Business Advisor
- **No Complex Forms:** An adaptive conversational consultant that asks only relevant questions based on business type, employee size, and operational setup.
- **Strict Data Grounding:** Powered by deterministic business logic tied directly to the real 25-product catalogue. The AI cannot hallucinate non-existent products or arbitrary pricing.
- **Contextual Explanations:** Every recommended line item includes a clear rationale (e.g., *"Cloud POS selected for UPI payments and GST invoicing"*).

---

### Slide 5: Intelligent Bundling & Transparent Pricing
- **All-in-One Basket:** Combines connectivity, communication, cloud productivity, security, and physical IoT into a single monthly bill.
- **Dynamic Customization:** Customers can adjust quantities, add optional modules (Wi-Fi, Toll-free, Endpoint Security), and immediately view updated pricing.
- **Zero Hidden Costs:** Transparent separation of Monthly Recurring Charges (MRC) and One-Time Setup Fees (OTC) with an itemized 18% GST breakdown.

---

### Slide 6: Smart KYC & Automated Verification
- **Digital-First Experience:** Direct in-browser upload of GST certificate, Udyam registration, Signatory ID, and Address Proof.
- **Automated Intelligence:**
  - Integrated OCR extracts GSTIN, dates, and enterprise names.
  - Mock Registry validation verifies active status against 3,534 GSTINs and 2,723 Udyam records.
  - Automated 90-day utility bill recency validation prevents invalid document submission.
- **Clear Categorization:** Clear outcomes (`VERIFIED`, `NEEDS_REVIEW`, `FAILED`, `MISSING_INFO`) with actionable remediation guidance.

---

### Slide 7: Omnichannel Distribution & Partner Empowerment
- **Augmenting, Not Replacing, Human Teams:** The platform empowers local channel partners and field sales agents with back-office intelligence.
- **Explainable Lead Propensity Scoring (0–100):** Prioritizes stalled leads based on GSTIN ownership, employee count, and spend intent so sales reps intervene where it matters most.
- **Partner Attribution:** Channel partners receive transparent commission tracking and lead ownership.

---

---

### Slide 9: Rollout Strategy & 6-Month Pilot Roadmap
- **Month 1–2 (Pilot in Tier-1 Hubs):** Launch digital self-serve platform across Bengaluru, Mumbai, and Delhi for Professional Offices and Micro Retail.
- **Month 3–4 (Channel Partner Integration):** Roll out the Back-Office Intelligence dashboard to top 250 Airtel channel partners across Tier-2 cities.
- **Month 5–6 (National Expansion):** Full-scale rollout across all 4 segments, integrating multi-lingual regional language support (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi).

---

### Slide 10: Governance, Security & Risk Mitigation
- **DPDP Act, 2023 Aligned:** Explicit consent logging, purpose-limited data storage, and strict document retention schedules.
- **Security Safeguards:** PII masking in registries, SHA-256 document hashing, role-based access control (RBAC), and server-side pricing validation.
- **Technical Resilience:** Deterministic fallbacks guarantee 100% platform availability even during external LLM or cloud API outages.
